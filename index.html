Music Genre Classification using Machine Learning

  • Machine Learning
  • Audio
  • MFCCs
  • Random Forest
  • CNN
  • LSTM

I explored a simplified approach to classifying music genres using the means and variances of Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (MFCCs). This project compares the performance of Random Forest Classifiers, CNNs, and LSTMs on a dataset of Rock, Electronic, Folk, and Classical music.

Capstone Project: Bipedal Locomotion using Reinforcement Learning

  • Reinforcement Learning
  • Unity
  • ML-Agents
  • PPO

This research explores the implementation of a reinforcement learning framework using the Unity engine and ML-Agents Toolkit to train a bipedal humanoid using a reward function and Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) algorithm. This project finds emergent behaviors, such as shuffling and extreme leaning, to demonstrate how agents exploit physics engines to find local optima rather than natural locomotion.

Hello!

I'm Sam, a computer scientist and data engineer at Collective Measures in Minneapolis. I graduated magna cum laude from Saint John's University in 2026 with a B.A. in computer science.

Outside of work, I love making and playing video games, storytelling, D&D, machine and reinforcement learning, robotics, art, and composing and producing electronic and orchestral music. And dragons, if you hadn't guessed.

I've trained digital bipedal humanoids to stand and walk, programmed self-driving RC cars using computer vision, and built procedural game generation in a few different programs.

I've played bass trombone for over 12 years in a couple of different wind ensembles and jazz bands, and I swam competitive sprint freestyle and butterfly for nearly a decade. I love to play D&D with friends, as both game master and player.

Right now, I'm working with a group of friends on Descent, my spin on a roguelike with heavy themes of corruption and divinity, where the player explores a lost ancient civilization.

Looking ahead, I'm getting ready for graduate school to get more hands‑on with robotics and intelligent systems. If you'd like to get in touch, feel free to reach out through email or on LinkedIn!
